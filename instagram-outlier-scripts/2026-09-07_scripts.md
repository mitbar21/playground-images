## Script 1 — "The best ad on Meta is 2 minutes of yapping"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DctiNrcvJri/ (@ericdoesecom — 37.5x their median). Format: split screen, talking head + example clip insert (top zone), ~50s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "The best performing ad on Meta right now is a two minute video of somebody yapping, and most of y'all are still cutting fifteen second UGC like it's 2023."

**Body:** "This format is everywhere right now and it prints because it doesn't look like an ad. It looks like a girl on her bed ranting about a problem. She goes a full minute and a half before the product ever shows up, and by the time it does, the viewer already agrees with everything she said, so the pitch doesn't feel like a pitch. And look, the AI part is easy now, you can generate the actor in an afternoon. The part everybody screws up is the script. If that rant isn't built on a pain your buyer actually lives with, you just made a two minute skippable ad. We rebuilt one of these for a client off their winning thirty second ad, held the product back, and cost per purchase dropped almost in half."

**Close:** "Long and boring is beating short and loud right now. Go test one."

*Visual note: example yapper-ad clip plays muted in top zone during body; caption lower third, white only.*

## Script 2 — "Ugly ads are outspending your designed ones"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DctjMkAxG9N/ (@kolton.hustles — 38.4x their median). Format: talking head + top-zone format mockup graphics, countdown, ~50s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "The ads printing the most money on Meta right now look like they cost zero dollars to make, and your polished branded creative is getting skipped."

**Body:** "Three formats crushing it right now. One, the zero star review. It reads like a bad review, people stop for the drama, then the review turns out glowing. Negativity gets the stop, the flip gets the sale. Two, the story reply. It looks like a creator answering a DM, so warm traffic reads the whole thing because it feels personal, not paid. Three, the notes app breakup letter. A petty little breakup note to your customers. Personal, weird, impossible to skip. All three work for the same reason, they hijack a format your brain already trusts, so the ad gets read before it gets judged. We ran an ugly notes-style ad against a client's designed carousel and the ugly one won on CPA. It wasn't close."

**Close:** "Go make one ad this week that your designer hates. Post it anyway."

*Visual note: each format shown as a top-zone mockup graphic (unique, specific), numbered 1/2/3 stat-style cards.*

## Script 3 — "Meta is shutting off unverified ad accounts"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcvJwJWy0iO/ (@kobyecommerceequation — 33.9x their median). Format: talking head + Business Manager screen recording insert, urgent PSA, ~50s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "Meta started killing unverified ad accounts this week, and most of you won't notice until your spend flatlines at zero."

**Body:** "September 7th was the line. If your Business Manager isn't verified, Meta can just stop serving your ads. No dramatic warning, just a quiet shutoff while you're wondering why delivery died. The fix takes ten minutes. Go into Business Manager, hit authorizations and verifications, and verify yourself as an organization. If Meta can't match your business to public records, you upload a bank statement or your business registration, and the name has to match your ad account name exactly. That mismatch is where most people get stuck. Then set your default advertiser and payer in the transparency section. We checked every client account this week and two of them weren't verified. Two. Real businesses, real money on the line."

**Close:** "Go check yours right now. Not after this reel. Now."

## Script 4 — "Meta built a pipe that lets Claude run your ads"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DctXZI8IJ-X/ (@saurabh_mangrulkar — 111.8K views, AI-in-ads lane). Format: talking head + screen inserts (MCP connect, dashboard), ~55s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "Meta built an official connector that lets Claude log into your ad account, and people are already letting it launch campaigns with zero adult supervision."

**Body:** "It's called the Meta Ads MCP and it's real. This is not some sketchy browser extension, it's Meta's own pipe. Claude can read every campaign, audit performance, pull competitor angles, draft creatives, even set budgets. We use it inside our agency every single day and it's honestly nuts how much grunt work it eats. But bro, the AI does not know your margins. It doesn't know your creative fatigued last Tuesday or that your offer is the actual problem. It will scale a garbage campaign with total confidence because the numbers looked fine for three days. The hands got automated. Knowing what to tell the hands didn't, and that still has to come from somebody who's watched a thousand accounts do weird shit."

**Close:** "Plug it in. Just keep a human on the kill switch."

## Script 5 — "I don't open Ads Manager in the morning anymore"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DctXZI8IJ-X/ (@saurabh_mangrulkar — reused, automation-routine angle). Format: single-shot walking monologue, ~45s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "I haven't started my morning inside Ads Manager in months, and my accounts have never been cleaner."

**Body:** "Every morning at six, a scheduled Claude routine logs into our ad accounts through Meta's official connector, pulls yesterday's spend, ROAS, cost per purchase by campaign, flags anything that moved more than it should, and drops the whole report in Slack before I've had coffee. That used to be forty five minutes of doom scrolling dashboards across every client. Now it's a two minute read. But listen, it flags, it doesn't decide. A dip can be creative fatigue, it can be seasonality, it can be your tracking quietly breaking, and the report can't tell those apart. Last month it flagged a dip that looked like fatigue and it was actually a busted pixel. The AI caught that something was off. Figuring out why took a person who'd seen it a hundred times."

**Close:** "Set the routine up this week. Easiest hour you'll ever get back."

*Visual note: walking single-shot, phone-height; one top-zone Slack-report screenshot pop mid-body.*

## Script 6 — "Your landing page is the leak, not your ad"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DctiNrcvJri/ (@ericdoesecom — reused, landing-page angle). Format: talking head + landing page screen pop (top zone), ~50s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "Your ad is not the reason your Meta campaign is bleeding, your landing page is, and y'all keep testing new hooks anyway."

**Body:** "Watch what the smart money is doing right now. They take the script from a winning ad, drop it into Claude, and have it build the landing page as a straight continuation of that ad. Same pain, same phrasing, same promise. So when somebody clicks, the page finishes the exact conversation the ad started instead of restarting it with generic homepage fluff. Most of y'all run a killer ad into a page that was written two years ago by whoever built the website, and then you blame Meta when it doesn't convert. That's not an ads problem. That's a message match problem. We did this for a client last month, didn't touch the ads at all, just rebuilt the page off the ad script, and conversion jumped enough to make scaling profitable again."

**Close:** "Fix the page before you kill another ad."

*Visual note: side-by-side top-zone graphic of ad copy next to matching page copy; highlight the repeated phrases.*
