## 6. Execution notes

- **Numbers are deliberately absent.** Every proof beat is qualitative ("we run this on client accounts all the time") because inventing metrics is banned. Drop your real numbers in at record time — scripts 1, 2 and 4 each have an obvious slot for one.
- **Profanity dial:** written profane-light per this skill's spec — one "bro," one mild line. Zero f-bombs. Say the word if you want it cleaner or heavier.
- **No comment bait anywhere**, even though 3 of the 5 source reels closed on it. Every close is a punch or an authority drop.
- **Agency-safe check:** script 4 is the risky one — it explicitly positions AI as the hands and a human as the one making the call, so it pulls toward the agency rather than replacing it. Script 3 does the same with "the tech is the easy part."
- **Record order:** 2 and 5 are the most time-sensitive (both are platform-change stories with a shelf life of days). Shoot those first.
- **Format rotation:** green-screen (1), overlay talking head (2), bare single-shot (3), split-screen screen-record (4), outdoor question-answer (5), raw ungraded phone (6).
