## Script 1 — "The Sharpie ad"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dcl-szZtDTT/ (@maazsiddiqii — 31.1x median, 62.9K views). Format: green-screen commentary over a screen recording, ~45s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "Somebody drew their car detailing ad on a piece of paper with a marker, took a photo of it, and it's beating every clean, polished, AI-generated ad you're running right now."

**Body:** "And most of you can't handle that, because you sat through a creative brief, a designer, and eight rounds of revisions, and the crayon drawing wins. It's not because ugly is magic. It's because every feed is wall-to-wall AI slop now. Same gradients, same fake studio lighting, same stock guy grinning at a laptop. Your brain filters all of it out before it even registers. The scribble doesn't look like an ad, so it doesn't get skipped. We run this on client accounts all the time and the sloppy one beats the beautiful one way more often than anybody wants to admit. So go draw your service on a napkin, shoot it with your phone, launch it as an ad today."

**Close:** "Your designer's gonna hate it. Run it anyway."

*Visual note: hold your own hand-drawn flyer up to camera in the first second. Then screen-record a real feed scroll of AI-perfect ads while you rant, so the contrast does the work.*

## Script 2 — "Meta took your placement controls"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcgDkAuhgun/ (@angad.loomba — 18.9x median, 30.2K views). Format: talking head with Ads Manager screenshot overlays, ~50s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "Meta is taking manual placement controls away from you, and half of you are about to let your budget leak straight into Audience Network and never even notice."

**Body:** "You've been unchecking those boxes at the ad set level for years. That's gone. And it's not the platform being evil, it's the same move they make every time: take the levers away, force it all into the algorithm, promise it'll sort itself out. Sometimes it does. Sometimes it quietly dumps your money into placements that have never sold you anything. Two ways to keep your hands on it. Value rules, where you bid down the junk placements and bid up the feeds where people actually buy. Or you block those placements account-wide in your account-level settings, so nothing leaks no matter what you launch later. Go do the account-level one first. It takes four minutes."

**Close:** "Then pull your last thirty days by placement. If you've been feeding Audience Network this whole time, you earned that."

*Visual note: screenshot the placement-settings notice on screen at the hook. Overlay the account-level settings path as you say it so people can follow along without pausing.*

## Script 3 — "Your five-second callback"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcfUl6agfPb/ (@nick_saraev_unfiltered — 49.2x median, 11.9K views, appeared in all four lanes). Format: single-shot talking head, mic in frame, ~50s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "Somebody fills out your form and you call them back four hours later. That lead is dead, and you already paid for it."

**Body:** "Speed to lead is the most boring thing in paid ads and it's the reason your cost per lead looks great while your cost per sale looks like garbage. What's working right now: form fill, five second timer on the thank you page, then an AI voice agent calls them. And the agent says it's an AI in the first sentence. Straight up. 'I'm an assistant, grabbing two quick things, then I'm connecting you to a real person.' Most people try to hide it. The lead clocks it in about nine seconds, feels lied to, and now you've bought a lead and burned it. Transparent AI buys your sales team the time it needs. Sneaky AI torches trust you paid real money for."

**Close:** "The tech is the easy part. Somebody still has to know what to ask. Go fix your callback time first."

*Visual note: no screen-share. Straight to camera, mic visible. Put a live countdown "5, 4, 3, 2, 1" on screen when you describe the timer.*

## Script 4 — "Let AI read your ad account"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dcb3-0Vyna6/ (@miranda.leadjourney — 21.1x median, 51K views, the AI-in-ads intersection). Format: split screen, talking head over screen recording, ~50s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "You can plug an AI model straight into your ad account now and ask it which campaigns actually made you money, and it'll answer in about nine seconds."

**Body:** "And I'm telling you to go do it. Ads Manager was built to show you clicks and cost per lead, which tells you almost nothing about whether the money came back. Connect it up and ask plain questions. Which campaigns produced leads that closed. Which ones produced tire kickers. What's my real cost per sale. It'll pull all of it. But then it hands you a very confident recommendation to kill an ad set, and it has no idea that ad set is your top of funnel, and killing it starves everything downstream about three weeks from now. It reads numbers. It doesn't know your business. It's the hands, not the brain."

**Close:** "Use it to get answers in seconds. Then let somebody who's actually scaled an account decide what to do with them."

*Visual note: screen-record the real connect flow and one real prompt, then cut to your face for the warning half. The cut is what sells it.*

## Script 6 — "If your ad looks like an ad"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dcl-szZtDTT/ (@maazsiddiqii — 31.1x median, 62.9K views; second angle off the week's strongest creative reel). Format: talking head, phone-shot, deliberately ungraded, ~45s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "If your ad looks like an ad, you already lost, and there is no budget number that fixes that."

**Body:** "Go look at what's actually winning in feeds right now. A screenshot. A bad phone photo. Somebody talking to their camera in a car with garbage audio. Meanwhile you're running the polished brand spot with the logo lockup and the swoosh. Nobody stops for that, bro. People stop for things that look like a friend posted them. And the second AI made perfect creative free, perfect creative became worthless, because now everybody has it. Scarcity moved. The human, sloppy, specific stuff is what's rare now. So go film one take on your phone, don't grade it, don't add the logo animation, drop it in the account against your current best performer."

**Close:** "It'll probably beat it. That should make you a little angry."

*Visual note: shoot this one deliberately raw, no color grade, auto captions only. The format is the argument.*
