## Script 1 — "Meta told us who's winning right now (it's not the budget)"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbCS2HyPqKP/ (@brendenbuilds — 199.2x median). Format: talking head + green-screen article/diagram, ~50s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "Meta just quietly told us why some advertisers are printing money right now and everybody else is getting smoked — and it's got nothing to do with how big your budget is."

**Body:** "Back on July 15th Meta's actual engineers — not the marketing team, the real engineers — put out how the new algorithm works. And the old Meta? It targeted what you did. You clicked a fitness ad, bought some protein, watched a dog video. The new Meta is building a whole fingerprint of who you actually are — like, health nut, two monitors, gets their best work done before 9am. So if your creative is still 'premium coffee, 20 percent off, here's our features,' you're feeding the old dead machine. We flipped a client's briefs last month, went from describing the product to describing the exact human — 'you're the founder color-coding your Notion at midnight still answering Slack' — and their cost per sale dropped, 'cause Meta finally knew who to hand the thing to. Describe the person. Not the product."

**Close:** "Meta already knows exactly who your customer is. Kinda wild your ads still don't."

*Visual note: talking head; at the reveal, green-screen the real "Engineering at Meta — POSTED JULY 15, 2026" headline behind you (screen-record over the shoulder). Top-zone stat pop: "what you clicked → who you are." Captions lower third.*

## Script 2 — "The 3-day rule I run on every account"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbNUPOktN5b/ (@omar_robert_tinsley — 102.1x median). Format: on-camera whiteboard, ~55s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "If you're scaling with paid ads and you're still letting a dead creative run a whole week hoping it wakes up, you're just lighting money on fire — so run the 3-day rule instead."

**Body:** "Start in ABO, five creatives, twenty bucks a day each. Not CBO — anybody telling a beginner to open in CBO is about to cost you a fortune. Then watch the clock. No click in the first 24 hours? Kill it. No add-to-cart by 48? Kill it. Nothing bought by 72 hours? It's gone. Whatever's still alive, and it's usually one or two, that's your winner, and now you graduate it up into a CBO or a cost cap depending on how that brand's been behaving, 'cause they all react different and you actually gotta test it, not copy some guru's screenshot. We run this across client accounts every single week. It's boring as hell, and it works."

**Close:** "The winners tell you fast. You just gotta stop babysitting the losers."

*Visual note: whiteboard, write ABO / 24–48–72 / CBO as you say each. Top-zone stat pops for the timeline. Captions lower third.*

## Script 3 — "AI can turn a garbage photo into a cinematic ad (here's the trap)"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbFZzJCy_8Q/ (@kushalfinds.ai — 24.7x median). Format: split-screen before/after + screen record, ~50s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "You can now take one garbage phone photo of your product and turn it into a full cinematic video ad in like ninety seconds — and that's exactly why a bunch of you are about to make worse ads, not better ones."

**Body:** "The tool's real, I'm not knocking it. Drop a product pic into one of these AI video generators, prompt it, and it spits out this glossy commercial that looks incredible. But nobody making these 'look what AI can do' videos tells you the ugly part: a pretty video is not an ad. Meta doesn't care that it's pretty, it cares that the right message hit the right person. So if you don't actually know who you're talking to or what makes them pull out a card, all AI does is help you crank out beautiful garbage faster, at scale. Use it to pump out volume once you've nailed the message. Don't use it to skip figuring out the message in the first place."

**Close:** "The video's free now, sure. Figuring out who you're even talking to — that part's still on you."

*Visual note: split-screen, plain product photo up top flipping to the cinematic AI ad below; you in the corner. Show the ugly-photo-to-ad flip on screen. Captions lower third.*

## Script 5 — "Why some advertisers are suddenly crushing it"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbCS2HyPqKP/ + https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbFZzJCy_8Q/ (cross-lane intersection — the week's hottest theme). Format: tight straight talking head, ~50s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "The people crushing it on Meta right now aren't smarter than you — they just clued into one thing: the new algorithm wants a ton of specific creative, and AI finally lets you actually feed it."

**Body:** "Two things smashed together this month. Meta rebuilt its targeting to understand who a person actually is instead of just what they clicked, which means more angles, more specific personas, more variations flat-out wins now. And at the same exact time these AI tools got good enough to crank out ten, twenty ad variations in the time it used to take you to make one. So the people winning are just letting AI pump out the volume Meta's been begging for. But — and this is the part the AI-hype crowd conveniently skips — pumping out a bunch of the wrong message just loses your money faster. Somebody still has to call which personas, which angles, which of those twenty are even worth spending on. Get that call wrong and the fancy AI just helped you lose money in half the time."

**Close:** "So yeah, let AI make you twenty ads. Just don't let it pick which ones to run — that still needs somebody who's done this a thousand times."

*Visual note: straight talking head, tight. Top-zone stat pops: "what you clicked → who you are" and "1 creative → 20 variations." Captions lower third.*

## Script 6 — "Stop killing campaigns on day three"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbNUPOktN5b/ (@omar_robert_tinsley — reused, paired with the new-algorithm angle). Format: talking head rant, ~45s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "Most of you launch a new campaign, watch it for like three or four days, panic at the first little dip, and shut it off — then go around telling everybody 'Meta ads don't work.'"

**Body:** "Nah, bro. You're just impatient. When a campaign kicks off, Meta grabs the easy low-hanging buyers first, then it starts working in the real audience, and with this new who-you-are algorithm it's literally still figuring out who your person even is — that takes a few days and way more than one touch before people move. You killing it on day three is like planting seeds and digging 'em up Thursday to check if they grew. Now if it's genuinely not converting after you gave it real time and decent creative, then fine, yeah — your creative's the problem, and no amount of budget fixes a weak message. But most of the time you didn't have a bad campaign. You just got twitchy and yanked it."

**Close:** "Give it the week. If it's still dead after that, go fix the creative — don't go blame Meta."

*Visual note: talking head, more animated/ranty. Top-zone timeline graphic: 24h → 48h → 72h → 7 days. Captions lower third.*
