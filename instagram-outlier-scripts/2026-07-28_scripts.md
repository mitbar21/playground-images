## Script 1 — "Meta told us who's winning right now (it's not about budget)"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbCS2HyPqKP/ (@brendenbuilds — 199.2x median). Format: talking head + green-screen article/diagram, ~50s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "Meta just quietly told us why some advertisers are printing money right now and the rest are getting smoked — and it's got nothing to do with your budget."

**Body:** "On July 15th Meta's actual engineers — not the marketing team, the engineers — published how the new algorithm works, and here's the part that matters. The old Meta targeted what you did: clicked a fitness ad, bought protein, watched a dog video. The new Meta is building a fingerprint of who you ARE — health-conscious, two monitors, hardest work happens before 9am. So if your creative is still 'premium coffee, 20% off, features features features,' you're feeding the old machine. We flipped a client's briefs last month from describing the product to describing the exact person — 'you're the founder who color-codes your Notion and answers Slack at midnight' — and their cost per purchase dropped, because Meta finally knew who to hand the ad to. Describe the human, not the product."

**Close:** "Meta's not guessing about your customer anymore. Stop making ads that guess about yours."

*Visual note: talking head; at the reveal, green-screen the real "Engineering at Meta — POSTED JULY 15, 2026" blog headline behind you (screen-record over the shoulder). Top-zone stat pop: "what you clicked → who you are."*

## Script 2 — "The 3-day rule we run on every ad account"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbNUPOktN5b/ (@omar_robert_tinsley — 102.1x median). Format: on-camera whiteboard listicle, ~55s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "If you're scaling with paid ads and you're still letting a dead creative run for a week hoping it wakes up, you're lighting money on fire — so here's the exact 3-day rule we run."

**Body:** "Start every test in ABO, not CBO — anybody telling a beginner to open in CBO is about to cost you money. Five creatives, twenty bucks a day each. Then the three-day rule: any creative with zero clicks in the first 24 hours, kill it. No add-to-cart by 48 hours, kill it. No purchase by 72 hours, it's gone. Whatever survives — and it's usually one or two — that's your winner, and now you graduate it into a CBO or a cost cap depending on how the brand behaves, because every brand reacts different and you have to actually test it, not copy some guru's screenshot. We do this across client accounts every single week. It's boring, and it works."

**Close:** "The winners tell you fast. You just have to stop babysitting the losers."

*Visual note: stand at a whiteboard, write ABO / 24–48–72 / CBO as you say each. Top-zone stat pops for the 24h/48h/72h timeline. Captions lower third.*

## Script 3 — "AI can turn a garbage photo into a cinematic ad (why that's a trap)"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbFZzJCy_8Q/ (@kushalfinds.ai — 24.7x median). Format: split-screen before/after + screen record, ~50s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "You can now turn one garbage phone photo of your product into a full cinematic video ad in about ninety seconds — and that's exactly why most of you are about to make worse ads, not better ones."

**Body:** "The tool's real. Drop a product photo into one of these AI video generators, prompt it, and it spits out a glossy commercial that looks incredible. But here's what nobody making these 'look what AI can do' videos tells you: a pretty video is not an ad. Meta doesn't reward pretty — it rewards the right message hitting the right person. So if you don't know who you're talking to or what actually makes them buy, AI just helps you produce beautiful garbage faster, at scale. The AI is the hands. The brain — who's this for, what's the angle, why do they care — that part's still on you, or on somebody who's done this ten thousand times. Use it to pump out volume once you've got the message. Not to skip figuring out the message."

**Close:** "AI made the production free. It didn't make the strategy free."

*Visual note: split-screen — plain product photo up top flipping to the cinematic AI ad below; you in the corner. Show the ugly-photo-to-ad transformation on screen. Captions lower third.*

## Script 5 — "Why some advertisers are suddenly crushing it"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbCS2HyPqKP/ + https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbFZzJCy_8Q/ (cross-lane intersection — the week's hottest theme). Format: tight straight talking head, ~50s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "The advertisers crushing it right now aren't smarter than you — they just figured out one thing: Meta's new algorithm wants VOLUME of specific creative, and AI finally lets you feed it."

**Body:** "Two things collided this month. Meta rebuilt its targeting to understand who a person is instead of what they clicked, which means more angles, more specific personas, more variations wins now — hands down. And at the exact same time, these AI tools got good enough to produce ten, twenty ad variations in the time it used to take to make one. So the operators winning are the ones letting AI crank the volume and diversity Meta's begging for. But — and this is the part the AI-hype crowd skips — volume of the wrong message just loses money faster. Somebody still has to decide which personas, which angles, which of those twenty are actually worth spending on. That judgment is the whole game. The AI's the printing press. You still have to know what to print."

**Close:** "Meta wants more shots on goal. Just make sure somebody's actually aiming."

*Visual note: straight talking head, tight. Top-zone stat pops: "old Meta: what you clicked → new Meta: who you are" and "1 creative → 20 variations." Captions lower third.*

## Script 6 — "Stop killing campaigns on day three"
*Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbNUPOktN5b/ (@omar_robert_tinsley — reused, paired with the new-algorithm angle). Format: talking head rant, ~45s.*

**Hook (on-screen + said):** "Most of you watch a new campaign for three or four days, panic at the first dip, and shut it off — then go tell everyone 'Meta ads don't work.'"

**Body:** "No, bro — you're just impatient. When a campaign launches, Meta grabs the low-hanging fruit first, the easy buyers, then it starts shuffling in the real audience — and with this new identity-based algorithm it's learning who your actual person is, which takes a few days and more than one touch before people move. Turning it off on day three is like planting seeds and digging 'em up Thursday to check if they grew. Now, if it's genuinely not converting after you've given it real time and real creative, then yeah — your creative's the problem, and no amount of budget fixes a weak message. But most of the time you didn't have a bad campaign. You had a nervous hand on the switch."

**Close:** "Give it the week. If it's still dead, go fix the creative — not the excuse."

*Visual note: talking head, more animated/ranty. Top-zone timeline graphic: 24h → 48h → 72h → 7 days. Captions lower third.*
